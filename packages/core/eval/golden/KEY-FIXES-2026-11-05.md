# Answer-key fixes, 2026-11-05

101 changes in 23 keys, so the summary metrics measure the pipeline rather than mistakes in the keys. Every new quote is word for word from `source/raw.txt`, and from the table-text copy in `v3.3.5-gate-2026-11-04/golden-tables` too. The README rules now also say that keys list only documents and sections the notice names, how rolling deadlines are labeled, and what eligibility leaves out.

## Judgment calls

The rules didn't settle these. Each needs a person to confirm or reverse it.

1. **Rolling grants still pass with a cutoff date.** IRAP's quarterly close dates, Gates's March 15 and September 1 review dates and NSF's target dates are now `other`, and each key has an `application` entry with `date: null`. When a key has no dated application deadline, `score.mjs` accepts any `other` date, so a summary that gives IRAP 2027-03-31 or Gates 2027-03-15 still scores correct. Only NSF moves (null now scores correct). Enforcing rule 3a needs a scorer change: accept only null when the key's application entry is dated null. That change wasn't made.
2. **Multi-date grants accept any application date, not only the last.** The NIH keys list every cycle, and CO Energy, WaterSMART, GOCO and DOE list several dates. The scorer counts any of them as correct. Holding the field to the last date needs a scorer change. The keys are unchanged.
3. **DOE Office of Science keeps 2027-09-30.** It takes applications at any time, but it closes on a stated date, which is the last date it accepts any application.
4. **DOD HIV/AIDS: 2027-09-18 is now `application`.** The announcement is "open to receive applications continuously until 5:00 p.m. ET, 18 September 2027"; each country narrative's 15 May 2026 date is kept as well.
5. **GOCO keeps 2027-03-11** even though urgent acquisitions are "accepted at any time". The regular cycles have fixed dates.
6. **Biosketches kept where the notice names them only weakly.** In K23 and NIDCD K18 the only mention is "collaborators and consultants do not need to provide their biographical sketches". GEMSSTAR says "should use the biographical sketch". NSF SaTC names the "Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section" in a "should" sentence. All four keep the item with that quote.
7. **Library sections the notice mentions only in passing are kept.** GEMSSTAR "Project Narrative" ("in their project narratives") and "Specific Aims" ("advance the specific aims"), and NIDCD "Specific Aims" (a review question). After the fix, I-START and the R21 parent list only the Resource Sharing and Data Management plans, and Clinical Informatics lists no library sections.
8. **Compliance: majority rule.** Most keys list no general compliance terms, and no NIH key lists the HHS antidiscrimination certification. So general "by applying you certify compliance with all executive orders" lines and Buy America were removed (Highlands, WaterSMART). Kept: compliance the notice puts under its own eligibility heading (Prop 68 "comply with Proposition 68…", R3 labor laws and CEQA). Also kept: status gates such as the MBTA Communities Act (Drought, LWCF), DOD's ROTC-access and confidentiality-agreement certifications, and the AFOSR research-security attestations.
9. **Match.** Added where `award.match.required` is true and eligibility lacked it: AmeriCorps, Highlands, IRAP, SS4A and WaterSMART. Not added for DOE, where cost share applies only to OTA awards, or for LWCF, whose key says no match although reimbursement is capped at 50%.
10. **Application types.** All 11 NIH keys already had one, Silvio included (eligibility[13], "Only New, Renewal, and Resubmission…"), so the reported Silvio gap didn't hold. The Silvio precision miss is a pipeline item about what Resubmissions may include. DOE was added. AFOSR was not, because "all applications are considered new" says how applications are treated, not which are allowed.
11. **Borderline amount and period items.** Removed: CDPHE's 80/20 core/support split and Highlands's "coordinate requests and stay under the maximum". Kept, as rules about who qualifies or what costs are allowed rather than award size: AmeriCorps's 20-member minimum, CO Energy's $5,000 limit on scholarship equipment, pre-award and performance-period cost rules (CDPHE, CO Energy, IRAP, LWCF, SS4A, WaterSMART's 3-year cost window), effort commitments, the DOW PI prior-funding limits, Silvio's research-base thresholds and the NIMH $500K contact rule.
12. **DOW $250K vs $300K: no change.** The key already lists both limits (eligibility[3] and [4]), each with `conflict: true`. Note that the scorer pairs them as two separate key items, so a summary with only one value loses one recall point. It does not "accept either", as the README says it should.
13. **Times and zones: no change.** Every timed deadline already gives the zone as written, or `null` where the notice states none.
